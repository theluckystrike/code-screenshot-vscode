# Frame, background and highlights

Open Options in the panel:

- Line numbers on or off. They are the real line numbers from the file.
- Frame: a window with dots, a plain window, or no window. Title is the file name or your own text.
- Background: five gradients, one color, the editor color, or transparent. Padding, Shadow and Corners.
- Remove common indent moves a nested block to the left edge. Wrap at wraps long lines at N columns.
- Mark text marks every match of a word.

Click a line to highlight it (Shift+click for a range). Double-click a word to mark it. Your choices are kept for the next picture.
