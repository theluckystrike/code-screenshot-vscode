# Copy code as an image

1. Select the lines you want. Partial lines are fine: whole lines are taken, so the indent stays right.
2. Press Cmd+Alt+C on macOS or Shift+Alt+C on Windows and Linux. You can also right-click and pick Copy as Image (PNG).
3. Paste. The PNG is on your clipboard, drawn with your editor theme, font, size and ligatures.

The panel beside the editor shows what was copied. Nothing to click, no website, no link.

If the clipboard cannot take the image (for example Linux without xclip or wl-clipboard), the panel says so, leaves your clipboard as it was and offers Save PNG.
